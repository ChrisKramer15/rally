# Requirements Document

## Introduction

A Flutter-based stock trading valuations engine that provides real-time market data, portfolio tracking, and trade recommendations. The application delivers predicted buy and short opportunities based on a supply and demand trading strategy, categorized by trade duration. Interactive candlestick charting with multiple timeframes and a visually striking neon-themed UI with light/dark mode support complete the experience.

## Glossary

- **App**: The stock trading valuations Flutter application
- **Portfolio_Tracker**: The module responsible for tracking user holdings and their current market value
- **Market_Data_Service**: The service that retrieves current market prices for stocks, ETFs, and cryptocurrency assets
- **Valuations_Engine**: The module that analyzes supply and demand patterns to generate predicted buy and short opportunities
- **Recommendation**: A trade suggestion generated by the Valuations_Engine, including direction (buy or short), trade category, and reward/risk ratio
- **Trade_Category**: A classification label for a recommendation: day trade, swing trade, or position trade
- **Reward_Risk_Ratio**: A numeric ratio expressing the potential reward relative to the potential risk for a given recommendation
- **Chart_View**: The graphical component displaying price data for an asset over a selected time duration
- **Candlestick_Graph**: A chart format displaying open, high, low, and close prices for each time interval as candlestick bodies and wicks
- **Time_Duration**: A selectable interval for chart data: 1 min, 5 min, 15 min, 30 min, 1 hr, 4 hr, 8 hr, 12 hr, 24 hr, 1 wk, 1 month, 1 qtr, 1 yr, or all-time
- **Theme**: The visual appearance mode of the App, either light mode or dark mode
- **Neon_Color_Palette**: The set of vibrant, high-saturation colors used throughout the App UI for visual emphasis and attractiveness

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Portfolio Tracking

**User Story:** As a trader, I want to track my portfolio holdings and see their current market value, so that I can monitor my investment positions at a glance.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user adds an asset to the portfolio, THE Portfolio_Tracker SHALL store the asset symbol, quantity (a positive numeric value between 0.0001 and 999,999,999 inclusive, up to 4 decimal places), and average purchase price (a positive value between 0.01 and 999,999,999.99 inclusive, up to 2 decimal places)
2. IF a user attempts to add an asset with an invalid symbol, a quantity outside the allowed range, or a purchase price outside the allowed range, THEN THE Portfolio_Tracker SHALL reject the entry and display an error message indicating which field is invalid
3. WHEN a user adds an asset that already exists in the portfolio, THE Portfolio_Tracker SHALL recalculate the average purchase price as the weighted average of existing and new quantities and update the total quantity
4. WHEN the portfolio view is opened, THE Portfolio_Tracker SHALL display each holding with its current market price, quantity, total value (current market price multiplied by quantity), and unrealized gain or loss ((current market price minus average purchase price) multiplied by quantity), each formatted to 2 decimal places
5. THE Market_Data_Service SHALL retrieve current market prices for all assets in the portfolio at the interval defined in Market Data Display requirements
6. WHEN an asset price is updated, THE Portfolio_Tracker SHALL recalculate the total portfolio value within 2 seconds
7. IF the Market_Data_Service fails to retrieve a price for more than 60 seconds, THEN THE Portfolio_Tracker SHALL display the last known price with a stale data indicator showing the time elapsed since the last successful update
8. IF the portfolio contains no holdings, THEN THE Portfolio_Tracker SHALL display an empty state message prompting the user to add an asset

### Requirement 2: Market Data Display

**User Story:** As a trader, I want to view current market prices for stocks, ETFs, and cryptocurrency, so that I can make informed trading decisions based on real-time data.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Market_Data_Service SHALL provide current price data for stocks, ETFs, and cryptocurrency assets
2. WHEN a user searches for an asset, THE App SHALL display matching results within 2 seconds with symbol, name, current price, and percentage change
3. WHEN a user enters fewer than 1 character in the search field, THE App SHALL not execute a search query
4. WHEN a user selects an asset, THE App SHALL display the current price, daily high, daily low, volume, and percentage change formatted to 2 decimal places for price values and 2 decimal places for percentage values
5. THE Market_Data_Service SHALL update displayed prices at a minimum interval of once per 60 seconds during market hours
6. IF the Market_Data_Service loses connectivity, THEN THE App SHALL display a connection status warning banner and retain the last received prices with a timestamp indicating when data was last updated
7. IF the search query returns no matching assets, THEN THE App SHALL display a message indicating no results were found

### Requirement 3: Valuations and Trade Predictions

**User Story:** As a trader, I want to see predicted buy and short opportunities based on supply and demand analysis, so that I can identify profitable trade setups.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Valuations_Engine SHALL analyze supply and demand zones to generate buy and short predictions for each tracked asset
2. WHEN a new prediction is generated, THE Valuations_Engine SHALL assign exactly one Trade_Category label: day trade (target hold duration less than 24 hours), swing trade (target hold duration between 1 day and 2 weeks), or position trade (target hold duration greater than 2 weeks)
3. WHEN a prediction is generated, THE Valuations_Engine SHALL calculate the Reward_Risk_Ratio for buy recommendations as (target price minus entry price) divided by (entry price minus stop loss price) and for short recommendations as (entry price minus target price) divided by (stop loss price minus entry price), displayed as a numeric value with two decimal places
4. WHEN a user views the valuations screen, THE App SHALL display all active recommendations sorted by Reward_Risk_Ratio in descending order
5. THE Valuations_Engine SHALL identify supply zones as price areas where selling pressure historically exceeded buying pressure, determined by at least 2 prior price rejections from the zone
6. THE Valuations_Engine SHALL identify demand zones as price areas where buying pressure historically exceeded selling pressure, determined by at least 2 prior price bounces from the zone
7. WHEN a price enters within 1% of a demand zone boundary, THE Valuations_Engine SHALL generate a buy recommendation if the Reward_Risk_Ratio exceeds 1.00
8. WHEN a price enters within 1% of a supply zone boundary, THE Valuations_Engine SHALL generate a short recommendation if the Reward_Risk_Ratio exceeds 1.00
9. WHEN a recommendation's target price is reached or stop loss price is reached, THE Valuations_Engine SHALL mark the recommendation as completed and remove it from the active recommendations list
10. IF there are no active recommendations for a given asset, THEN THE App SHALL display a message indicating no current trade setups are available

### Requirement 4: Trade Recommendation Display

**User Story:** As a trader, I want recommendations clearly labeled by trade duration and reward/risk ratio, so that I can select trades matching my trading style and risk tolerance.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a recommendation is displayed, THE App SHALL show the asset symbol and name
2. WHEN a recommendation is displayed, THE App SHALL show the Trade_Category label as one of: "Day Trade", "Swing Trade", or "Position Trade"
3. WHEN a recommendation is displayed, THE App SHALL show the Reward_Risk_Ratio formatted as "R:R X.XX" where X.XX is the ratio value
4. WHEN a recommendation is displayed, THE App SHALL show the direction as "BUY" or "SHORT"
5. WHEN a recommendation is displayed, THE App SHALL show the entry price, stop loss price, and target price formatted to 2 decimal places
6. THE App SHALL visually distinguish recommendations by Trade_Category using distinct Neon_Color_Palette colors
7. IF a recommendation has incomplete data (missing entry price, stop loss, or target price), THEN THE App SHALL not display the recommendation and SHALL log a data integrity warning

### Requirement 5: Chart Display with Candlestick Toggle

**User Story:** As a trader, I want to view price charts with a toggle to switch to candlestick format, so that I can analyze price action using my preferred chart style.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user views an asset chart, THE Chart_View SHALL display a line chart plotting the close price for each time interval by default
2. WHEN a user activates the candlestick toggle button, THE Chart_View SHALL switch the display to a Candlestick_Graph within 1 second
3. WHEN a user deactivates the candlestick toggle button, THE Chart_View SHALL switch back to the line chart display within 1 second
4. THE Chart_View SHALL retain the selected chart type (line or candlestick) for the duration of the user session
5. WHILE displaying a Candlestick_Graph, THE Chart_View SHALL render each candle with a body representing the open and close prices and wicks representing the high and low prices
6. THE Chart_View SHALL color bullish candles (close above open) and bearish candles (close below open) with two visually different Neon_Color_Palette colors
7. THE Chart_View SHALL visually indicate the currently active chart type on the toggle button so the user can identify whether line or candlestick mode is selected
8. IF the Chart_View has fewer than 2 data points available for the selected Time_Duration, THEN THE Chart_View SHALL display a message indicating insufficient data instead of rendering the chart

### Requirement 6: Time Duration Selection

**User Story:** As a trader, I want to select from multiple time duration options for charts, so that I can analyze price action across different timeframes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Chart_View SHALL provide the following selectable Time_Duration options: 1 min, 5 min, 15 min, 30 min, 1 hr, 4 hr, 8 hr, 12 hr, 24 hr, 1 wk, 1 month, 1 qtr, 1 yr, and all-time
2. WHEN a user selects a Time_Duration, THE Chart_View SHALL reload the chart data for the selected interval within 3 seconds
3. WHILE chart data is being retrieved, THE Chart_View SHALL display a loading indicator overlaid on the chart area until the data is fully loaded or an error occurs
4. THE Chart_View SHALL visually distinguish the currently selected Time_Duration option from unselected options using the Neon_Color_Palette
5. WHEN a user changes the Time_Duration, THE Chart_View SHALL maintain the current chart type selection (line or candlestick)
6. WHEN the Chart_View is first displayed, THE Chart_View SHALL default to the 24 hr Time_Duration option as the active selection
7. IF chart data fails to load within 3 seconds or a network error occurs, THEN THE Chart_View SHALL display an error message indicating the data could not be retrieved and SHALL retain the previously displayed chart data

### Requirement 7: Light and Dark Mode

**User Story:** As a user, I want to switch between light and dark mode, so that I can use the app comfortably in different lighting conditions.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE App SHALL provide a toggle control to switch between light Theme and dark Theme
2. WHEN the user selects dark Theme, THE App SHALL immediately apply a dark background with light text and Neon_Color_Palette accent colors across all screens without requiring an app restart
3. WHEN the user selects light Theme, THE App SHALL immediately apply a light background with dark text and Neon_Color_Palette accent colors across all screens without requiring an app restart
4. WHEN the user toggles the Theme, THE App SHALL persist the selected Theme preference to local storage within 1 second of the selection
5. WHEN the App is launched for the first time, THE App SHALL default to dark Theme
6. IF the persisted Theme preference is unreadable or corrupted, THEN THE App SHALL default to dark Theme

### Requirement 8: Neon Visual Design

**User Story:** As a user, I want the app to have a vibrant neon color scheme, so that the interface is visually attractive and data elements are easy to distinguish.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE App SHALL use the Neon_Color_Palette for primary UI accent elements including buttons, chart lines, and active indicators
2. THE App SHALL use distinct neon colors to differentiate buy recommendations (green-spectrum neon) from short recommendations (red-spectrum neon)
3. THE App SHALL use neon color highlights for positive percentage changes and a contrasting neon color for negative percentage changes
4. THE App SHALL maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 between text and background colors and a minimum contrast ratio of 3:1 between non-text UI components (buttons, icons, chart elements, active indicators) and their adjacent colors in both light and dark Theme
5. THE App SHALL apply neon glow effects to interactive elements (buttons and toggle controls) such that the text contrast ratio of the element remains at or above 4.5:1 with the glow effect active
6. THE App SHALL differentiate buy from short recommendations and positive from negative percentage changes using at least one visual cue in addition to color, such as directional icons or text labels, to support colorblind users
