# Supply & Demand — One-Page Cheat Sheet

## A valid zone = BASING + STRONG MOVE AWAY
Not just highs/lows. Trade the **first touch** of a **fresh** zone.

| Pattern | Demand | Supply |
|---|---|---|
| Reversal (Peak/Valley) | Drop-Base-Rally | Rally-Base-Drop |
| Continuation (mid-trend) | Rally-Base-Rally | Drop-Base-Drop |

## Draw the zone
- **Proximal line** = closest to price · **Distal line** = furthest from price.
- Basing: tight, **1–4 candles** (fewer is better).
- Distal at lowest wick of basing (demand) / highest wick (supply). Don't cut candles.
- Use TradingView **magnet tool** for precision.
- **Move away = most important.** Want A+: explosive, large body, little/no wick (marubozu).

## Trade plan
- **Entry:** pending **limit order** just before the proximal line.
- **Stop:** a little **below** the zone (never on the distal line). Never move it wider.
- **Target:** fixed R:R (2:1, 3:1, 5:1) · opposing zone · prior structure · trail stop.
- **Manage:** leave it / break-even / **half-risk** (favorite) after a set R:R.

## Multiple timeframe (odds booster)
1. Map zones on **higher timeframe**.
2. Wait for price to reach the HTF zone.
3. Drop to **entry timeframe**, take a fresh zone in the same direction.
- Price is **fractal** — same method on all timeframes.
- Swing: 1h/daily, 4h/weekly · Day: 1m/15m, 5m/1h, 15m/4h.
- Higher timeframe always wins. Beginners: **swing trade**.

## Zone quality checklist
- [ ] Strong / A+ move away
- [ ] Tight 1–4 candle basing
- [ ] **Fresh** (untested)
- [ ] Breaks an opposing zone OR removes a trend line
- [ ] Aligned with higher-timeframe trend
- [ ] Bonus: liquidity trap (S/R just before the zone)

## Psychology
- Losing streaks (4–8) are normal. Know your break-even win rate (2:1 → ~33%).
- Simulate/backtest to learn max & average drawdown.
- No revenge trading. No strategy hopping. No FOMO.
- Realistic goal: **20–30%/year**. Beating 8–10% is already good.
- Learn to trade first — money follows. Keep your day job.
